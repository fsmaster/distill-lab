How it was made

Part two

Anti-distillation: stopping someone from doing this to your model

What I did above is exactly what a competitor does to a paid model API: ask it thousands of questions and train a cheap copy on the answers. So I turned the teacher into a "protected API", wrote an attacker script that harvests it, and measured each defense on the same budget, same student and same test.
